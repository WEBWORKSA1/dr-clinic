What we do

  • Semantic headings, landmarks and a “skip to main content” link on every page.
  • Keyboard-accessible menus, forms, tools and video players, with visible focus outlines.
  • Text and interface colours chosen for strong contrast, in both light and dark modes.
  • Forms with visible labels, clear error messages and no time limits.
  • Animations reduced automatically if you have turned on “reduce motion”.
  • Plain-language writing, short paragraphs and descriptive link text.
  • Pages that work when zoomed to 200% and on small screens.

Known limitations

Some embedded third-party content, such as YouTube videos and advertisements, is controlled by its providers and may not fully meet our standard. Captions are available on most embedded videos through the YouTube player.

Report a barrier

If anything on dr.clinic is hard to use, please tell us through the contact page. Let us know the page and the problem, and we will try to fix it or provide the information another way.
