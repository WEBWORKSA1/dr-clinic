PharmacistMedicine questions; minor ailments and emergency supplies in many regionsMinutesLowest

Not sure? Call a nurse line. NHS 111 (England), 811 (most Canadian provinces, including Info-Santé in Québec), healthdirect 1800 022 222 (Australia), or the 24/7 nurse line printed on many insurance cards. Mental-health crisis: 988 in the US and Canada.
