How the US Navy method works

The method, developed by the US Naval Health Research Center, estimates body fat from circumference measurements and height. Men's estimates use the waist and neck; women's use the waist, hips and neck, because women tend to store more fat around the hips.

Ranges commonly published by the American Council on Exercise. Healthy ranges also vary with age.

Getting reliable numbers

Measure in the morning before eating, wear light clothing or none, and stand relaxed. Track the trend every 2–4 weeks rather than reacting to a single number. If you are concerned about weight loss or gain you did not expect, see your doctor.
