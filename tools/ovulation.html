How the estimate works

Ovulation usually happens around 14 days before your next period starts, not 14 days after the last one. So with a 28-day cycle it is around day 14, but with a 32-day cycle it is around day 18. The calculator applies this rule to your average cycle length.

Signs that can help

  • Cervical mucus often becomes clear, slippery and stretchy near ovulation.
  • Ovulation predictor kits detect the hormone surge that comes 24–36 hours before ovulation.
  • Basal body temperature rises slightly after ovulation, which confirms it in hindsight.

When to see a fertility specialist

See a clinician if you have been trying to conceive for 12 months without success, or after 6 months if you are 35 or older. Seek advice sooner if your periods are very irregular or absent, or if you or your partner have a known condition that may affect fertility.
