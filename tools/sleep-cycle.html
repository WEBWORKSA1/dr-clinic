How sleep cycles work

During the night you move through repeating cycles of light sleep, deep sleep and REM (dreaming) sleep. Each cycle lasts roughly 90 minutes, though it varies from about 70 to 120 minutes. Waking near the end of a cycle, rather than in deep sleep, may help you feel more alert. The calculator also allows about 15 minutes to fall asleep.

Habits that matter more than timing

  • Keep the same wake-up time every day, including weekends.
  • Get daylight in the morning and dim lights in the evening.
  • Avoid caffeine after early afternoon and large meals or alcohol close to bedtime.
  • Keep your bedroom cool, dark and quiet, and keep screens out of bed.

When to see a clinician

Talk to your doctor about loud snoring with pauses in breathing or gasping, falling asleep during the day, trouble sleeping on most nights for three months or more, or restless, uncomfortable legs at night. These can be signs of treatable sleep disorders such as sleep apnea or insomnia.
