How to measure accurately at home

  1. Avoid caffeine, exercise and smoking for 30 minutes before.
  2. Empty your bladder, then sit quietly for five minutes with your back supported and feet flat.
  3. Rest your arm on a table at heart level and use a cuff that fits your upper arm on bare skin.
  4. Do not talk. Take two readings one minute apart and record both.
  5. Measure at the same times each day — for example morning and evening — for a week before an appointment.

Why one reading is not enough

Blood pressure changes minute to minute with stress, activity and even talking. Diagnosis is based on the average of several readings on different days. Our log keeps your last readings on this device so you can show the average to your clinician.
