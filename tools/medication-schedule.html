When do you take it? *

Getting the most from a medicine schedule

  • Copy instructions exactly from the pharmacy label or prescription.
  • Link doses to daily habits — breakfast, brushing teeth, bedtime.
  • Use a weekly pill organiser alongside the printed schedule.
  • Include over-the-counter medicines, vitamins and herbal supplements, which can interact with prescriptions.
  • Bring the schedule to every appointment and to the pharmacy when you start something new.

Ask your pharmacist

Pharmacists are medicine experts and are often the easiest health professional to reach. Ask them to review all of your medicines at least once a year, or whenever a new one is added, to check for interactions, duplicates and doses that could be simplified.

Never stop or change a prescribed medicine without talking to your prescriber. If you think you or someone else has taken too much of a medicine, contact your local poison control centre or emergency number straight away.
