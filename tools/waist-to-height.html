What the numbers mean

RatioMeaning for adults
0.4 to 0.49Healthy central weight
0.5 to 0.59Increased health risk
0.6 or moreHigh health risk

These categories come from UK NICE guidance, which recommends that adults keep their waist to less than half their height. They work for adults of all ethnicities and both sexes, which makes the ratio easier to use than waist cut-offs alone.

Using it with BMI

Waist-to-height ratio adds information that BMI misses. Someone with a “healthy” BMI can still carry risky central fat, and a muscular person with a high BMI may have a healthy ratio.

What helps

Regular activity, fewer sugary drinks and ultra-processed foods, more fibre, enough sleep and limiting alcohol all help reduce waist size over time. If your ratio is 0.5 or more, ask your doctor about checking your blood pressure, blood sugar and cholesterol.
