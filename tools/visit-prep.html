Why preparing helps

Appointments are short, and it is easy to forget important details or questions under pressure. Writing them down — and leading with your most important concern — helps your clinician focus on what matters most to you. Research and patient-safety organisations consistently encourage patients to prepare questions and bring a medicine list.

During the visit

  • Start with your main concern in one or two sentences.
  • Be specific about timing, severity and what you have tried.
  • Ask the clinician to explain any terms you do not understand.
  • Before leaving, repeat the plan back in your own words (“teach-back”).
  • Ask who to contact if things get worse, and when to follow up.
