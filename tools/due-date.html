Pregnancy at a glance

StageWeeksCommon appointments
First trimester0 to 13 weeks 6 daysFirst prenatal visit, blood tests, dating scan, first-trimester screening
Second trimester14 to 27 weeks 6 daysAnatomy scan around 18–22 weeks, glucose screening around 24–28 weeks
Third trimester28 weeks to birthMore frequent checks, growth monitoring, birth planning

Pregnancy is counted from the first day of your last period, about two weeks before conception, so at conception you are already considered “two weeks pregnant”.

Next steps

  • Book a prenatal appointment with a doctor, midwife or obstetrician. Many people are seen first around 8 weeks.
  • Take a daily supplement containing folic acid (usually 400 micrograms) unless your clinician advises a different dose.
  • Review your medicines with a pharmacist or doctor — do not stop prescribed medicines without advice.

Get urgent care for heavy vaginal bleeding, severe abdominal pain, fainting, a severe headache with vision changes, fluid leaking from the vagina, or if your baby is moving less than usual later in pregnancy.
