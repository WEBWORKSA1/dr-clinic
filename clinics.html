Red flags to walk away from

  • Guaranteed results or “miracle” cures
  • Pressure to pay in full today or sign up for a package
  • No clear information about who will treat you, or their qualifications
  • Refusal to give a written estimate or to share your records
  • Reviews that all sound the same, or no independent reviews at all
  • Treatments that skip a proper consultation and consent discussion
