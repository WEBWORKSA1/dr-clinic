# dr.clinic — Research Summary (November 2026)

## The number that decides the model
At the same traffic, **patient lead generation earns roughly 5–8× more than AdSense**.

- Health RPM is about $10–$25.
- Healthcare cost-per-lead benchmarks run from $19 (dermatology) and $32 (urgent care) up to $107–$450 for dental implants. The average is around $162.

So AdSense covers costs and the matching funnel makes the profit.

## Features adopted on dr.clinic
- **Care navigator.** Taken from Cleveland Clinic "Where to go", healthdirect and Solv.
- **Specialist and clinic-type finders.** Taken from Zocdoc, Healthgrades and WhatClinic.
- **15 private calculators and records.** Taken from WebMD, Healthline, Drugs.com and Patient.info.
- **Multi-step match funnel with consent**, the "List your clinic" B2B funnel, pay-per-lead and featured plans. Taken from Bookimed, Zocdoc, Healthgrades and Sesame.
- **Trust layer:** editorial policy, sources on every guide, accessibility statement, emergency prominence, anti-scam note. Taken from NHS, MNT, Healthline and Kaiser.
- **Donations, advertising, careers and contests.** Taken from Mayo/Cleveland "Give", WebMD "Advertise", and Lybrate/Teladoc recruitment.

## Trademark check
"DR. CLINIC" is a live USPTO registration (Serial 79291759, Reg. 6,258,855, Classes 3 and 5), owned by a cosmetics company. Our response:
- the service is branded "Care Navigator";
- "dr.clinic" is used only as the domain;
- no goods are sold;
- a disclosure is published at `/trademark/`.
