How we hire

  1. Application

    We read every application and reply within two weeks.

  2. Short paid trial

    A small, paid sample task that reflects the real work.

  3. Conversation

    A short video call about how you work and what you want to build.

  4. Offer

    Clear scope, rate and expectations in writing.

Scam warning: dr.clinic will never ask applicants to pay a fee, buy equipment or share banking details during hiring.

We welcome applicants of every background, identity and ability, and we judge applications on skills and work samples.
