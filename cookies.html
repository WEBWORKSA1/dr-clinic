We do not use our own analytics or advertising cookies, and the information you type into our health tools is never placed in cookies.

Managing your choices

  • Consent message: where required, a Google consent message lets you accept or reject personalised advertising. Reopen it any time with the “Privacy & cookie choices” link in the footer.
  • Google ad personalisation: turn it off at Google Ads Settings.
  • Industry opt-outs: aboutads.info (US) and youronlinechoices.eu (Europe).
  • Your browser: you can block or delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Some features, such as saved tool data, will then stop working.

For more on how we handle information, read our privacy policy.
