Where should you go for care?

Choosing the right setting saves time and money — and gets you the right level of care.

Use the care navigator

Emergency department

Chest pain, trouble breathing, stroke signs, severe injury or bleeding, seizures, severe allergic reactions.

Urgent care

Same-day, not life-threatening: sprains, minor cuts, fevers, ear or urinary infections, rashes.

Primary care

Checkups, long-term conditions, screenings, prescriptions and referrals to specialists.

Telehealth

Video visits for minor illnesses, refills, follow-ups and many mental-health appointments.
