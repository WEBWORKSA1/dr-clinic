Contest rules — key terms

  • No purchase necessary. Payment never increases your chances of winning.
  • Eligibility: 18 or older; employees of dr.clinic and of a contest's sponsors, and their households, are not eligible. Void where prohibited or restricted by law.
  • Official rules first: each contest publishes full official rules — dates, eligible countries, prize description and approximate value, judging criteria and odds where relevant — before entries open. Those official rules take priority over this summary.
  • One entry per person per contest round unless the official rules say otherwise.
  • Winners are contacted by email and must respond within 14 days, or an alternate may be selected. Where required by law, winners may need to answer a skill-testing question.
  • Prizes are non-transferable. We may substitute a prize of equal or greater value. Winners are responsible for any taxes.
  • Your content: you keep ownership of your entry and grant dr.clinic a non-exclusive licence to display it with credit.
  • Health safety: challenges are for generally healthy adults. Check with your clinician before changing your activity if you have a medical condition.
  • Privacy: entry details are used to run the contest. See our privacy policy.

Want to sponsor a prize? Your brand is credited on the contest page and winner announcements. Sponsor a contest or fund a prize.
