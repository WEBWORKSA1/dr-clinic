General information only

The content on dr.clinic — including text, graphics, videos, calculators, the care navigator and clinic suggestions — is provided for general informational and educational purposes only. It is not intended to be, and is not, a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Always seek the advice of your physician or another qualified health provider with any questions you may have about a medical condition. Never disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking it because of something you have read or calculated on this website.

No doctor–patient relationship

Using dr.clinic, submitting a form or receiving clinic suggestions does not create a doctor–patient, nurse–patient or any other healthcare relationship with dr.clinic.

Tools and calculators

Our tools use published formulas and general reference ranges. Results are estimates and may not be accurate for you — for example during pregnancy, for children and teenagers, for older adults, for athletes, or for people with medical conditions or taking certain medicines. Use results as a starting point for a conversation with a clinician, not to make treatment decisions.

Accuracy and currency

We work to keep content accurate and up to date and list our sources, but medical knowledge and guidelines change and practice differs between countries. We make no guarantee that content is complete, current or applicable to your situation.

Clinics and third parties

Clinics suggested through our matching service are independent. We do not employ their clinicians, and we do not guarantee their services, availability, prices or outcomes. Always check a clinician's registration and ask questions before agreeing to treatment.

Advertising, sponsorship and clinic-fee disclosure

  • Sponsored tools, hubs, newsletter slots and contest prizes are labelled as sponsored.
  • Advertisers, sponsors and partner clinics have no control over our health content.
