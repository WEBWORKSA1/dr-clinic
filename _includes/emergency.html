Call your local emergency number now for chest pain or pressure, trouble breathing, signs of stroke (face drooping, arm weakness, speech difficulty), severe bleeding, a seizure, fainting or unresponsiveness, a severe allergic reaction, or thoughts of harming yourself or others.

911 US & Canada · 999 UK · 112 EU & India · 000 Australia · In the US and Canada you can call or text 988 for a suicide and mental-health crisis line.
