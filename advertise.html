Placements

Every sponsored placement is labelled. Media kit and current audience figures on request.

Sponsored tool

“Presented by” placement on a calculator such as BMI, blood pressure or due date — high repeat usage.

Specialty or city hub

Exclusive sponsorship of a specialty hub (for example dental or fertility) or a city care page.

Newsletter sponsor

A clearly marked sponsor slot in the weekly “Know where to go” email.

Contest and prize sponsor

Fund a prize for a challenge or the Clinic Hero Awards and be credited throughout.

Video and content partnership

Co-produced explainers that meet our editorial standards, labelled as partner content.

Display advertising

Programmatic display through Google AdSense, or direct-sold placements for qualified brands.

Our advertising standards

  • Every paid placement is labelled “Sponsored”, “Presented by” or “Partner content”.
  • Advertisers and sponsors have no control over our health guides or recommendations.
  • We do not accept ads with misleading health claims, miracle cures or unapproved treatments.
  • Regulated products and services must hold the approvals required where they are advertised.
  • We never sell visitors' personal health information to advertisers.
  • We may decline any advertiser for any reason.
